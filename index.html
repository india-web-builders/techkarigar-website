FAQ

Questions Answered

How long does it take to build a website?

Most websites are delivered in 1–4 weeks depending on complexity. A simple business website typically takes 1–2 weeks. E-commerce or custom web apps take 3–6 weeks. We always provide a clear timeline before starting.

Do you work with small and local businesses?

Yes, that's mostly who we work with. We know what it takes to get a local business online and we've done it for 100+ businesses across India.

What is office automation and how does it help?

It means we write scripts that take over the boring, repetitive stuff your team does by hand. Data entry, report generation, file sorting, email workflows. Most clients save 3 to 8 hours a day.

Can you build apps for both Android and iOS?

Yes. Native Android, native iOS, or cross-platform apps that run on both from one codebase. The cross-platform option is usually cheaper and ships faster, which works well for most businesses.
