FAQ

Questions Answered

How long does it take to build a website?

Most websites are delivered in 1–4 weeks depending on complexity. A simple business website typically takes 1–2 weeks. E-commerce or custom web apps take 3–6 weeks. We always provide a clear timeline before starting.

Do you work with small and local businesses?

Absolutely — small and local businesses are our specialty. We understand the unique challenges of taking a local business online and we've helped 100+ businesses across India do exactly that.

What is office automation and how does it help?

Office automation means custom scripts and tools that handle repetitive tasks automatically — data entry, report generation, file processing, email workflows, and more. Our clients typically save 3–8 hours of manual work per day.

Can you build apps for both Android and iOS?

Yes. We build native Android and iOS apps, as well as cross-platform hybrid apps that work on both platforms from a single codebase — which is more cost-effective for most businesses.

How do I get started?

Simply email us at info@techkarigar.com with a brief description of your project. We'll respond within 24 hours with a free consultation.
